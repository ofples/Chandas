## Short description

Flexible timers and gentle bells for work, movement, meditation and rest.

## Full description

Chandas helps you keep a steady rhythm without watching the clock.

Create a simple repeating timer, build a routine with named steps, or let gentle bells run continuously. Keep several timers running together and return to the one you need from a clear, visual library.

Make each routine your own:
- Choose bells and gongs, device sounds, or an audio file you import.
- Add repeating or random sub-bells, or place bells at a percentage of an interval or a chosen time before its end or after its start.
- Follow the countdown on a circular timer, with colors and marks for upcoming bells.
- Run continuously, for a number of rounds, for a duration, or until a chosen time.
- Align intervals to the clock or let them run freely.
- Set active hours for audible cues, adjust individual sound levels, or mute until a chosen time.
- Enable an alarm that rings until dismissed, with lock-screen alerts when Android permits them.
- Keep an ongoing timer notification, with optional live countdown and floating timer on supported devices.
- Save configurations, keep edits as drafts, and import or export routines.
- Start with optional Pomodoro, workout and meditation templates and a short guided tour.

The running controls are yours to choose. Show the ones you use, hide the rest, and enable optional dial gestures to adjust time or move between steps. Light, dark and e-ink themes keep the interface calm.

Chandas works in the background and retains active timers across app restarts. Alarm delivery and lock-screen presentation depend on Android permissions and device settings.

No account, advertising or analytics. Your timer settings and sessions stay on your device. The app makes limited network requests to Expo to check for application updates; see the privacy policy for details.

Set your rhythm, put the phone down, and let Chandas mark the passage of time.

# Chandas Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 13, 2026**

Chandas is a timer application provided by Yukti ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how Chandas handles information when you use the application.

## Summary

Chandas does not require an account and does not collect personal information for Yukti. It does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell user data.

Timer settings and session state are stored locally on your device. Chandas makes limited network requests to Expo's update service to check for and download application updates.

## Information stored on your device

Chandas stores the following information in the application's private storage:

- Main and sub-interval settings
- Snap and reset configuration
- Volume and temporary mute settings
- Active-hours configuration
- Alarm and focus-mode preferences
- Theme and advanced-settings preferences
- The state and timing of an active timer session

This information is used only to provide the timer's features and restore an active session after the app or device restarts. It is not uploaded to Yukti or shared with other users.

Local information remains on your device until you change it, clear Chandas's app data, or uninstall Chandas.

## Network communication and application updates

Chandas uses Expo Application Services to check for and download application updates. When an update check occurs, limited technical information is transmitted to Expo over HTTPS. This can include the app version, runtime version, operating-system platform, update identifiers, and standard connection information such as an IP address and request metadata.

This information is used only to select, secure, and deliver the correct application update. Yukti does not use it for advertising, profiling, or analytics. Expo processes this information as our service provider under [Expo's Privacy Policy](https://expo.dev/privacy).

Chandas does not otherwise send your timer settings or session activity over the internet.

## Device permissions and system access

Depending on your device and the features you enable, Chandas may request or use the following system capabilities:

- **Notifications:** To show timer status, interval events, and alarm alerts.
- **Exact alarms and full-screen alarm notifications:** To deliver time-sensitive alarms and display them over the lock screen when alarm mode is enabled.
- **Background operation and wake locks:** To keep an active timer working when the app is not visible or the screen is locked.
- **Start after device restart:** To restore an active timer after the device reboots.
- **Do Not Disturb access:** If you enable a focus session and grant access in Android settings, Chandas creates and controls an automatic Do Not Disturb rule that allows alarms. Chandas does not read the contents of your notifications.
- **Audio controls:** To play bells, gongs, and alarm sounds. Chandas does not record audio or access your microphone.

You can revoke notifications, alarms, or Do Not Disturb access at any time in Android settings. Some related features may stop working after access is revoked.

## Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information for advertising. Limited technical update-request information is processed by Expo solely to provide application updates, as described above. We may disclose information if required by law, but we do not possess your locally stored timer information.

## Data security

Chandas relies on Android's application sandbox to protect locally stored settings and uses HTTPS for update requests. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit processing to what is necessary to operate and update the app.

## Data retention and deletion

Yukti does not maintain user accounts or a server-side database of Chandas timer activity. You can delete all information stored by Chandas by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Expo retains technical service data according to its own privacy policy and retention practices.

## Children's privacy

Chandas is a general-purpose timer and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Chandas's features or legal obligations change. The revised policy will be published at this location with a new effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Yukti using the developer contact details displayed on Chandas's Google Play listing or open a private support request through the contact mechanism provided there.
